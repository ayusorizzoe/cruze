SALOME

the room ↗

Salome is an artist-run space in Brackwater, established in 2017. We work across sound, moving image, installation, and a lot of things we didn’t imagine we’d end up doing.

Over the years, we’ve become part of each other’s lives in ways that have very little to do with art. We share meals, look after one another, get on each other’s nerves, and sometimes spend entire afternoons together without getting much done. It’s a place we keep coming back to, even when we don’t have a reason to.

We open our doors for exhibitions, screenings, performances, and other events. Outside of those occasions, Salome is a working space, but we’re always happy to meet people interested in what we do. Get in touch if you’d like to visit.
