access to the space

Rob and Grace · Rev. 2026

Salome

Access to the space

For members, collaborators, and anyone using the building outside public hours.

Getting in

The front door is usually locked outside scheduled events.

If you’re visiting, arrange a time with someone from Salome beforehand. Please don’t assume somebody will be there just because the lights are on.

If you’ve been given a key, don’t make copies or pass it to anyone else without checking with the group.

Opening up

Turn on the lights you need. The switches for the front room are beside the entrance.

Check that the back door is closed before leaving the building unattended.

If you’re expecting visitors, make sure someone is available to let them in.

Using the rooms

The front room is used for exhibitions, screenings, performances, and gatherings. Please check the shared calendar before setting anything up there.

The back room is a working space. You’re welcome to use the tables, but leave enough room for other people to work.

The upstairs room is generally quieter. If someone is working there, ask before bringing a group upstairs.

Don’t move or dismantle someone else’s work without asking.

Equipment

Projectors, speakers, recorders, cables, and other shared equipment are kept in storage.

If you take something out of the building, write down what you’ve borrowed, where it’s going, and when you expect to return it.

Please check with the group before lending equipment to anyone outside Salome.

If something stops working, tell someone. Don’t put it back on the shelf and hope nobody notices.

Courtyard

The courtyard is available for working, taking breaks, and spending time outside.

Please cover the workbench when you’re finished, especially if rain is expected.

Don’t leave materials against the fence or block the back door.

If you’re working with something that produces fumes or dust, be mindful of people inside and in the neighboring buildings.

Closing up

Before leaving, please:

  1. Put away shared equipment and return furniture to where you found it.
  2. Check the upstairs window, particularly if it’s been raining.
  3. Make sure the kitchenette is clean and the refrigerator is closed.
  4. Turn off lights and any equipment that shouldn’t be left running.
  5. Lock the courtyard door and front entrance.

If you’re the last person leaving, you’re responsible for checking the whole building.

Something wrong?

If you notice a leak, broken lock, electrical problem, or anything that might make the space unsafe, tell the group as soon as possible.

If something needs fixing, please don’t assume someone else has already mentioned it.

One last thing

Salome is a shared space. Some people come here to work, others to spend time together, and often it’s both.

Please be considerate of whoever else is here, and leave the building in a condition that makes it easy for the next person to use.

Prepared by Rob and Grace
Revised as needed
