Public hearing transcript Community Recreation Programming Proposal to rename the annual Father & Son Fishing Day at Laguna Honda Wildlife Park. Town Council · October 20, 2026 · Item 3 · Excerpt Open transcriptClose transcript

Town of Brackwater · Town Council

Public Hearing: Community Recreation Programming

October 20, 2026 · 6:00 p.m. · Council Chambers, Town Hall

Excerpt. Presiding: Mayor Ashford. Present: Council Members, Seats 1 through 4; Town Manager; Town Clerk. Council Members are identified by seat.

Item 3. Public hearing: proposal to rename the annual Father & Son Fishing Day, Laguna Honda Wildlife Park.

THE MAYOR We’ll open the public hearing on Item 3. The Clerk will read the item.

THE CLERK Item 3. A proposal to rename the annual Father and Son Fishing Day at Laguna Honda Wildlife Park. Placed on the agenda at the request of Seat 3.

THE MAYOR Seat 3.

SEAT 3 Thank you. I’ll be quick. The proposal is to call it something that describes who actually comes. Laguna Honda Fishing Day. Brackwater Fishing Day. I’m not attached. That’s the proposal.

THE MAYOR Thank you. Seat 1.

SEAT 1 Mr. Mayor, I’ll be honest, I didn’t think we’d ever be having this conversation. Father and Son Fishing Day is part of who this town is. It’s heritage. You don’t rename your heritage every time the wind changes.

SEAT 3 How long has it been running?

THE MAYOR Through the chair, please.

SEAT 3 Mr. Mayor, how long has it been running?

THE MAYOR Clerk?

THE CLERK The first Father and Son Fishing Day was held eleven years ago.

SEAT 3 Eleven.

SEAT 1 Eleven years is a long time.

SEAT 3 My truck is eleven years old.

SEAT 1 And you’d be upset if somebody tried to rename it.

[Laughter.]

MEMBER OF THE PUBLIC Sorry. Excuse me. Is this going to take long? I’m here about the toilet at the boat ramp.

THE MAYOR You’ll have your opportunity under general public comment. That’s Item 5.

MEMBER OF THE PUBLIC It’s been out of order since July.

THE MAYOR Item 5. Thank you. Seat 4.

SEAT 4 I just want to understand what we’re talking about. Who’s actually allowed to sign up?

THE CLERK Registration is through Parks and Recreation. The form asks for one adult and one child.

SEAT 4 Can daughters come?

THE CLERK Yes.

SEAT 4 Do they?

THE CLERK Yes.

SEAT 2 Mothers?

THE CLERK Yes.

SEAT 2 Mothers with daughters?

THE CLERK Yes.

SEAT 4 What about a kid who doesn’t have a father? Who comes with an aunt, or a neighbor?

THE CLERK Yes. The form asks for one adult and one child.

SEAT 4 Does the form ask whether the adult is the child’s father?

THE CLERK No.

SEAT 4 Does it ask whether the child is a son?

THE CLERK No.

[Pause.]

SEAT 2 Then what makes it Father and Son Fishing Day?

THE CLERK That is the name of the event.

SEAT 3 Thank you.

SEAT 1 That isn’t nothing.

SEAT 2 For the record, I don’t care what it’s called. I’d like it moved to April. It was forty-eight degrees out there last year.

THE CLERK Fifty-one.

SEAT 2 It was windy.

THE MAYOR If I may. When I was a boy, my father was a very busy man. He was serving this state. But one Saturday he took me out on the water, just the two of us, and showed me how to bait a hook. I have carried that morning with me my whole life. Nobody is saying a mother can’t take her child fishing. But there is something between a father and his son that I believe is worth naming. This town chose to name it. I don’t believe we should be ashamed of that.

SEAT 3 Nobody’s ashamed. It’s a fishing day.

THE MAYOR It is a good deal more than a fishing day, and I think everyone here knows it.

SEAT 3 Then what is it?

THE MAYOR It’s Father and Son Fishing Day.

MEMBER OF THE PUBLIC I’m sorry. I’m sorry, but there are kids at that boat ramp every weekend. Fathers, sons, daughters, whatever you end up calling them. And there is nowhere for them to go. They go in the reeds.

THE MAYOR We are not on that item.

MEMBER OF THE PUBLIC I know which item we’re on.

[Gavel.]

SEAT 2 What about Family Fishing Day?

SEAT 1 Every day out there is a family fishing day. A name that describes every day doesn’t describe anything.

SEAT 3 Parks and Recreation has had calls. People asking whether they’d be welcome. Single moms. Grandparents. People who see the name and figure it isn’t for them.

SEAT 4 Who called?

THE CLERK The office does not record who calls.

SEAT 3 One of them was me. My daughter saw the flyer and asked if she was allowed to go. I didn’t know. So I called.

SEAT 1 Anyone who wants to come is welcome. The name tells them so.

SEAT 3 The name tells them something.

SEAT 1 Can I tell you something? The first year they held it, I took my father. He was eighty-one. He couldn’t cast anymore, so I cast for him and he held the rod. Somebody from Parks took our picture for the newsletter. That picture is on my refrigerator. I’m his son. It counted.

SEAT 3 Nobody’s taking the picture off your refrigerator.

SEAT 1 It says Father and Son Fishing Day on the banner behind us.

[Pause.]

SEAT 2 Mr. Mayor, I’d move that we continue this item until staff can report back on the event’s formal eligibility requirements.

THE CLERK The eligibility requirements are one adult and one child.

SEAT 2 The formal ones.

THE CLERK Those are the formal ones.

SEAT 2 In a report, then.

SEAT 1 Second.

MEMBER OF THE PUBLIC I have been here since six o’clock. I filled out the card. I called Public Works four times and they told me to come to the meeting. I came to the meeting.

THE MAYOR And we are grateful that you did.

MEMBER OF THE PUBLIC You don’t need a report on who the toilet is for. It’s for whoever needs it. It just has to work.

THE MAYOR There is a motion and a second. All in favor?

[Seats 1 and 2 and the Mayor in favor. Seats 3 and 4 opposed.]

THE MAYOR The motion carries. Given the hour, the chair will entertain a motion to adjourn.

MEMBER OF THE PUBLIC Item 5—

THE MAYOR Will carry over to the next regular meeting. Thank you all for your patience, and God bless.

[Gavel.]

Action. Item 3 continued to a date to be determined, pending a staff report on the formal eligibility requirements of the event. Motion carried, 3–2 (Seats 3 and 4 opposed). The event will be held in the spring under its current name.

Item 5, general public comment, was not reached. The meeting adjourned at 9:47 p.m. One speaker card (restroom, Laguna Honda Wildlife Park boat ramp) is retained with this record and has been referred to Public Works.

Agreement Laguna Honda Industrial Water Service Agreement Supply of water from Laguna Honda Reservoir to the Tessera Systems facility in the industrial district. Town of Brackwater and Tessera Systems · Executed September 12, 2023 · Excerpt Open agreementClose agreement

Laguna Honda Industrial Water Service Agreement

between the Town of Brackwater (“the Town”)
and Tessera Systems (“the Company”)

Executed September 12, 2023

Excerpt. Schedules A, B, and D are maintained separately by the Town Clerk.

Recitals

A.The Town owns and operates Laguna Honda Reservoir (the “Reservoir”), whose capacity exceeds the Town’s present municipal demand.

B.The Company intends to operate a computing facility in the Town’s industrial district (the “Facility”) and requires a reliable supply of cooling water.

C.The Town wishes to encourage investment and employment in the industrial district while maintaining the Reservoir as a public resource.

1. Industrial Water Supply

1.1Supply. The Town shall supply the Facility with untreated water from the Reservoir for cooling and related operational purposes, up to the annual volume set out in Schedule A.

1.2Municipal priority. During a declared water emergency, supply to the Town’s residential customers shall take priority over supply to the Facility.

1.3Declaration. A water emergency may be declared only when the Reservoir falls below the minimum operating level set out in Schedule A, as shown by the monitoring data reported under Section 4.4.

1.4Notice. Outside a declared water emergency, the Town shall not reduce supply to the Facility without ninety (90) days’ written notice to the Company.

2. Intake Works and Maintenance

2.1Construction. The Company shall construct, at its own cost, an intake, pumping station, and conveyance pipeline between the Reservoir and the Facility boundary (the “Intake Works”). Ownership of the Intake Works shall pass to the Town on completion.

2.2Maintenance. The Town shall operate, maintain, and repair the Intake Works. The Company shall reimburse the Town for that work at the rates set out in Schedule B, adjusted annually by no more than three percent (3%).

2.3Access. The Company and its contractors shall have access to the Intake Works at all times.

3. Shoreline Access

3.1Restricted areas. The Town shall close to the public the portions of the Reservoir shoreline shown in Schedule C as Restricted Shoreline Areas, and shall be responsible for their fencing, signage, and enforcement.

3.2Public access. The Town shall keep Area 2 of Schedule C open to the public where consistent with the safe operation of the Intake Works. The Company may ask the Town to close Area 2 on forty-eight (48) hours’ notice for maintenance or operational reasons.

3.3Future needs. Where an expansion of the Intake Works requires it, the Company may request the designation of additional Restricted Shoreline Areas. The Town shall not unreasonably withhold approval.

3.4Signage. Signage for Restricted Shoreline Areas shall be prepared in accordance with Section 4.3.

4. Environmental Stewardship and Public Communications

4.1Shared interest. The Parties acknowledge a shared interest in the long-term health of the Reservoir.

4.2Shoreline planting. The Company shall fund native planting and shoreline maintenance within and adjacent to the Restricted Shoreline Areas, in the amounts set out in Schedule D.

4.3Public Education and Community Engagement. The Town recognizes the Company as a partner in reservoir stewardship initiatives. The Town shall consult the Company in preparing educational materials, interpretive signage, and public communications concerning the Reservoir’s water management, its shoreline, or the Facility, and shall give reasonable consideration to the Company’s comments.

4.4Monitoring. Reservoir level and water quality at the Intake Works shall be monitored by the Company and reported to the Town each quarter.

5. Term

5.1Term. This Agreement shall remain in effect for twenty-five (25) years and shall renew for successive ten-year terms unless either Party gives notice of non-renewal no later than two (2) years before expiry.

For the Town of Brackwater
Mayor Dale Ashford
For Tessera Systems
Authorized representative
Attest
Town Clerk

Schedule C · Restricted Shoreline Areas (excerpt)

Area 1
Intake Works, pumping station, and the shoreline within one hundred (100) feet of either. Closed to the public.
Area 2
Shoreline path between the public boat ramp, Laguna Honda Wildlife Park, and Area 1. Open to the public, subject to Section 3.2.

Note on Area 2, from the negotiation file

The Town’s draft of June 2023 proposed that Area 2 remain open to the public at all times, citing its use for fishing and community events. The Company objected that the conveyance pipeline runs beneath the shoreline path, and that unrestricted access could not be reconciled with its security and insurance requirements. The Parties agreed to the wording of Section 3.2.

The Town also asked that the boat ramp be excluded from any future designation under Section 3.3. The request was not adopted.
