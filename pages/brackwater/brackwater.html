Resident Services

Water
Municipal water comes from Laguna Honda Reservoir and other regional sources. Planned interruptions are posted in Latest Notices. Report leaks or service problems to Public Works through the municipal offices.
Roads and Drainage
Public Works maintains town roads, ditches, and culverts. Report blocked drainage or damaged roads to the municipal offices. During flooding emergencies, call emergency services.
Permits
Building, development, and land use permits are issued by Planning and Permits. Applications are available from the municipal offices.

Statement from the Office of the Mayor

Regarding Recent Inquiries into My Personal Life

November 13, 2026

To the people of Brackwater,

For six years, I have had the privilege of serving this town, guided by my faith in Almighty God, my devotion to my family, and my belief that the strength of our community begins in the home.

Read the full statement

Recently, members of the press have approached my office concerning matters of a deeply personal nature. I had hoped to address these circumstances privately, with the dignity my family deserves. However, I will not allow speculation to speak on my behalf.

I have been involved in a relationship outside my marriage. This was a failure of personal judgment, for which I take full responsibility before my wife, my family, and God.

I ask for their forgiveness.

But I also ask the people of Brackwater to remember that a man’s failures do not erase a lifetime of service.

There are those who will use this moment to question the values upon which I have built my public life. To them, I say that the institution of marriage is not weakened by the failures of an individual. It is strengthened by our willingness to recognize those failures and return to the principles that have sustained our families for generations.

Under my administration, we have welcomed new investment, restored activity to our industrial district, and brought opportunity to a town that too many others had written off. These accomplishments were not achieved through perfect men, but through men willing to answer a higher calling.

I wish to make clear that no municipal funds have been used for personal accommodations, travel, or related expenses.

I will not resign.

Brackwater has made too much progress to abandon the work we began six years ago. I intend to continue serving this town, defending the values that have guided my administration, and working toward the future that Brackwater deserves.

I ask for your prayers, for my family, and for the continued prosperity of our community.

May God bless you, and may God bless Brackwater.

Environmental Stewardship · Laguna Honda Reservoir

Protecting Laguna Honda for Future Generations

November 10, 2026

The Town of Brackwater remains committed to preserving Laguna Honda Reservoir, one of our community’s most treasured natural resources. Through continued shoreline maintenance, habitat protection, and responsible water management, we are ensuring that the reservoir remains a source of pride for generations to come.

We are especially pleased to recognize Tessera Systems for its partnership in advancing Brackwater’s environmental and economic future. The company’s ongoing industrial water operations represent an important step toward making responsible use of the reservoir’s full potential.

Portions of the shoreline remain closed to the public to support these efforts. Residents are reminded that unauthorized access to restricted areas may result in penalties.

A healthier reservoir. A stronger Brackwater.

Community Events

  • Brackwater Harbor Festival

    Annually in the fall · Waterfront

    The annual waterfront arts festival, with installations and illuminated cranes, warehouses, and industrial structures along the working harbor.

    The 2026 festival ran on selected evenings from October 9 to November 1, with projections by the local collective Salome.

  • Father & Son Fishing Day

    Spring · Laguna Honda Wildlife Park

    A longstanding community fishing day at Laguna Honda Wildlife Park. Rods and bait provided. Registration opens in the spring through Parks and Recreation.

  • Town Council Meetings

    Council Chambers, Town Hall

    Regular meetings are open to the public. Residents may speak during public comment, in the order in which items are heard. Hearing transcripts are published in Public Records.
