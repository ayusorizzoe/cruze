Town Government

Brackwater has a council–manager government. Residents elect the Mayor and four council members. The Town Council sets policy, adopts the budget, and appoints a Town Manager to run the Town’s day-to-day operations.

Office of the Mayor

Mayor Dale Ashford

Mayor of Brackwater · Second term

For six years, Mayor Ashford has led Brackwater with the conviction that a strong town is built on strong families. A man of deep Christian faith, he and his wife, Cynthia, have made their marriage and their church the foundation of a life in public service.

The Mayor was raised in a family devoted to that service. His father, Warren Ashford, served as Governor of the state.

Under his leadership, Brackwater has welcomed new industrial investment, most notably the Tessera Systems facility, approved during his first term, which has brought renewed activity to the industrial district and new confidence to the local economy.

Now in his second term, the Mayor continues to champion economic revitalization and the values that have sustained Brackwater for generations. He believes the town’s best years are still ahead.

“Faith, family, and service are not three separate commitments. They are the same one.”
