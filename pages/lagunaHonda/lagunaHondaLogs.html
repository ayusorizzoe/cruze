November 6, 2026

Location: Reservoir shoreline

Keeper: Ray

water’s down another inch since last week, and i checked the south gauge twice to be sure. saw two black-necked stilts near the intake making enough noise for six birds, which was nice considering how few we’ve had lately.

a woman stopped me and asked if i remembered her father, who used to work on the pumps. good mechanic, terrible card player, still owes me fourteen dollars. she told me he’d died in February, and that he never stopped talking about this place even after moving away. she wanted to see the old pump house, so i told her she could walk as far as the fence. watched her go down the path until i couldn’t see her anymore.

the younger one came in around two with a haircut. told him it looked fine, and he said that wasn’t much of a compliment. i told him he hadn’t asked for one, which apparently means i’m getting worse with age.

probably.

October 24, 2026

Location: North marsh trail

Keeper: Danny

spent twenty minutes removing a grocery bag from a buttonbush, the roots had grown through one of the handles. a month ago that whole bush would’ve been standing in water, and now it’s in dry mud. two boys were fishing nearby, and one asked where the alligators were. i said we don’t usually see them here, but apparently his uncle saw one last week, so who knows.

he asked if i could teach him to tie his line because his dad had shown him but he’d forgotten, and for some reason that made me really happy. his friend kept telling us we were doing it wrong, despite not knowing how himself. they asked if my dad taught me, and i said i’d learned from a video at seventeen, which they thought was hilarious.

i showed them twice, then made them try. one caught a little croaker and held it up like he’d won something, so i congratulated him before telling him to put it back. he did, eventually.

October 22, 2026

Location: Park office

Keeper: Ray

spent the morning reading old water measurements while the younger one typed them into a spreadsheet. he says we’re missing three years, and i told him we’d managed fine before computers, which he seemed to find very funny considering the missing records.

we got to july 2001, when my daughter used to come to work with me. she was twelve and thought the whole place smelled terrible, and apparently she’d been writing comments in purple pen beside my measurements. july 16 said “smells bad,” july 17 said “mosquitoes,” july 18 said “dad looked at a heron for 20 minutes,” and the next day she’d written “still the heron.”

i laughed at that last one, hadn’t remembered it at all. she died nine years ago, and i don’t think i’ve opened this folder since. the younger one asked if i wanted to stop, but i told him no, i was keeping the pages though. he said he only needed the numbers, so we started august.

October 20, 2026

Location: Maintenance shed

Keeper: Ray

the shed door’s been catching on the frame again, so the younger one helped me take it off the hinges. he kept telling me to hold it higher, and i kept telling him there wasn’t any higher left to hold it.

he’s better with the drill than i am, which i mentioned, and somehow we got talking about who taught us to use tools. i asked if his father had shown him, and he said no, he’d mostly figured things out himself. i asked if there were other things he’d missed out on doing as a boy.

he looked at me for a moment, then said there were plenty, but fixing things wasn’t one of them. i wasn’t sure what to say to that, so i asked him to pass me the screwdriver.

he handed me the drill.

said i’d been holding the screwdriver the whole time.

October 18, 2026

Location: Visitor center

Keeper: Danny

spent the morning replacing the trail notices near the visitor center, most of them had faded so badly you could barely read them. someone had written “nice try” underneath the sign asking people not to feed the birds, which i left for now.

a certain artist came by after lunch to use the printer because the one at the collective broke again, carrying invoices and a bag of oranges. i asked if the oranges were for me, and she said no but gave me one anyway. the festival wants another revision to her projections because apparently the cranes look too industrial. i asked what they expected cranes to look like, and she said she’d asked the same thing.

we sat outside while she ate another orange, and she told me a studio in another city wanted to see her work. i asked if she’d sent it, and when i asked when, she told me i sounded like her mother.

later she said she liked my shirt, which she’s said about the last three. apparently i’m supposed to take a hint.

anyway, the printer worked.

October 14, 2026

Location: Western access road

Keeper: Ray

someone dumped a mattress beside the western access road, with a stain on one side i’d rather not identify. public works said they could collect it Thursday, but the younger one wanted to move it out of the ditch before the rain, even after i told him twice we weren’t touching it.

he went and got the wheelbarrow anyway, and somehow i ended up helping him. we’d moved it about six feet when the bottom tore open and a family of mice came running out, sending him backward so fast he nearly fell into the ditch. i laughed so hard i had to sit down, which he didn’t appreciate.

he told me i was being an asshole, and he was right, although i don’t think he would’ve done much better if it had been me.

we left the mattress where it was. public works can get it Thursday.

October 8, 2026

Location: South observation platform

Keeper: Danny

two great blue herons, one green heron, and a lot of mosquitoes this morning. a man asked me to photograph him and his wife, but he didn’t like how his stomach looked and wanted one where he looked like he used to. she told him she’d married him twenty-eight years ago and knew exactly what he used to look like, which made him laugh. i took another, and they stayed on the platform for a while watching the herons.

i was checking the railing when my phone buzzed. someone had sent me another picture from the other night, taken on her couch while i was trying to hide my face in her lap. i told her to stop sending those while i was working, and she sent three more. the last one was actually pretty good, so i asked her for it without the others.

she said they were all good. i told her the railing needed fixing, which was true, although it didn’t have much to do with what we were talking about.
