Brackwater Operations

Built on what remains.

Brackwater has a long history of making room for what comes next. Its harbor, industrial buildings, and freshwater infrastructure were developed for a future that arrived differently than expected. Much of that capacity remains.

Tessera’s Brackwater facility brings existing infrastructure into new use, supporting advanced computing operations while creating employment and investment opportunities in the region.

Computing infrastructure

Built for continuous operation.

The work performed here extends well beyond the facility itself. Information travels between distant locations, processing demands change, and the systems supporting them must remain available.

Our Brackwater facility provides computing capacity for Tessera’s security technologies and commercial AI clients. Its infrastructure supports model development, data processing, and continuous operations.

Water and resources

A shared resource.

Water has shaped Brackwater’s development for generations. Laguna Honda was expanded when the town expected to grow, and its capacity became part of a future that never fully materialized. Today, that same water supports new possibilities.

Tessera operates under an agreement with the municipality governing water access from the reservoir for facility cooling. We coordinate with local authorities to support reliable operations and responsible resource management.

Community

A future close to home.

For many communities, opportunity has meant leaving. Young people grow up knowing that the work they hope to do may exist somewhere else. Those who stay build their lives around what remains available.

Tessera contributes technical employment, educational partnerships, and local investment. Our education initiatives support students at schools across the region, including Moses Catholic School, Brackwater High School, and Harbor Technical Institute, through technology grants, equipment funding, and opportunities to explore careers in computing and engineering.

We believe communities should have opportunities to participate in emerging industries without having to leave the places they call home.

Opportunity shouldn’t require leaving home.
