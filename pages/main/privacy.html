Privacy

Your searches are your business.

About this website

Cruze is part of Brackwater, a fictional town presented through a collection of interconnected websites.

Although these websites appear to belong to different organizations, they are all part of a single creative project.

Information and tracking

Brackwater does not intentionally track visitors, collect search histories, or build personal profiles.

You don’t need to create an account or provide personal information to explore the website.

How search works

Cruze’s search function operates within the project. Your searches are used to find matching content and are not used to personalize future results.

Cookies and third-party services

Brackwater does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics to track visitors.

Depending on how the website is hosted, the hosting provider may process basic technical information, such as IP addresses, to deliver the website.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address in order to deliver them.

External links

Some pages may contain links to websites outside Brackwater. Those websites operate independently and may have different privacy practices.
