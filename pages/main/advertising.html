Advertising

Put yourself on the map.

Brackwater is a fictional town made up of interconnected websites, and there’s room for more.

We’re inviting trans and nonbinary people to create something of their own that could exist within the town.

It might be a business, a community organization, an advertisement, a personal website, a photograph, a fictional document, or something we haven’t imagined yet.

Make something of your own

Brackwater is interested in how we experience the world around us, what we find funny, what frustrates us, what we care about, and what we wish were different.

Your contribution can draw from your experiences, but it doesn’t have to be autobiographical. You’re free to invent, exaggerate, fictionalize, and experiment.

Your work doesn’t need to be explicitly about gender, and you aren’t expected to speak for anyone other than yourself.

There’s no particular perspective or conclusion you need to arrive at.

How contributions work

You create the work, and we discuss together how it might exist within Brackwater.

Contributions can be small or ambitious. You don’t need to build an entire website to participate.

Submissions are reviewed before being included, and not every submission can be accepted.

Your work stays yours

You retain ownership and authorship of everything you create.

If your contribution is accepted, you’ll be asked for permission to display it within Brackwater. You’ll be credited according to your preferences.

We won’t substantially alter, adapt, or repurpose your work without your agreement.

Contributors can also request removal of their work.

Interested in contributing?

We’d love to hear what you’re imagining.

Submission details will be available here when contributions open.
