Help

Need a hand finding something? Here are a few ways to get started with Cruze.

What is Cruze?

Cruze is the search interface for Brackwater, a fictional town presented through a collection of interconnected websites.

Although these websites appear to belong to different organizations, they’re all part of the same creative project.

You can explore them in any order. There’s no particular place you need to start.

What should I search for?

You can search for places, organizations, services, or subjects that interest you.

Not sure where to start? Try searching for reservoir, art, wildlife, or technology.

You don’t need to know the name of a website to find it.

Why am I not getting any results?

Cruze searches a limited collection of websites created for Brackwater, so some searches won’t have a match.

Try using a different word or a broader search term.

If we can’t find a match, we’ll also show you other websites you can explore. They might not answer your original question, but you’re welcome to visit them.

How do I open a website?

Select any result to visit the website.

You can also choose a suggestion while typing, without finishing your search.

Can I search for something outside Brackwater?

You can enter any search you like, but Cruze only searches websites included in the project. It isn’t connected to the wider internet.
