How Search Works

Cruze helps you find websites and information from across our region. Our index is small, but there’s plenty to explore.

Finding results

Start typing in the search bar to see suggestions from our index. You can select a suggestion or press Enter to view your results.

Cruze searches website names, descriptions, and page content, so you don’t need to know the name of a particular website to find it.

What can you find?

Our index includes public services, local businesses, community projects, independent publications, and other websites from around the region.

You might find information about a place you’re curious about, an upcoming event, a local organization, or something you weren’t looking for in the first place.

When there’s no match

We don’t have an answer for every search.

If we can’t find anything matching your query, we’ll let you know and suggest other websites you can explore. These suggestions may not be related to what you originally searched for.

You can also try a broader term or search for a different subject.

How results are organized

Cruze aims to show results that are relevant to the words you search for. Matches in website names, descriptions, and page content help determine which results appear.

Our index is still growing, so results may be limited.
