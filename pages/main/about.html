About Cruze

Cruze is an independent search engine built to help people find websites, businesses, and information across our region.

We started Cruze because we believed finding local information should be easier. Not every business has a large online presence, and not every useful website appears at the top of a national search.

Our search index brings together local organizations, public services, businesses, and independent websites, making them easier to find in one place.

We’re a small team, and our index is still growing. If you don’t find what you’re looking for, try another search or browse the websites currently available.

Thanks for searching with us.
